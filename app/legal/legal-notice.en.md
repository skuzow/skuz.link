## 1. Who runs skuz.link

I, Alejandro Porras, run skuz.link from Spain. You can write to me at [contact@skuz.link](mailto:contact@skuz.link).

skuz.link is a personal, non-commercial project: it earns no income and shows no ads.

## 2. What skuz.link is

skuz.link is a free URL shortener. Anyone who signs in can create links that redirect to other web addresses.

## 3. Source code

The code of skuz.link is free software under the [GNU GPL v3](https://github.com/skuzow/skuz.link/blob/main/LICENSE) and is available at [github.com/skuzow/skuz.link](https://github.com/skuzow/skuz.link). The license applies to the code.

## 4. Links to other sites

The links on skuz.link are created by its users. I don't check in advance the addresses they redirect to, and I don't answer for what they lead to.

I remove any link that breaks the [Terms of use](/terms) as soon as I learn about it. To report one, write to [abuse@skuz.link](mailto:abuse@skuz.link).
